## FAQs

**Can you help with a destination wedding?** Yes, we coordinate vendors for destination weddings
across our active cities. A relationship manager will call to understand the plan. **[CONFIRM]**

**Can I get just one vendor, e.g. only a photographer?** Yes — single-category requests are welcome.

**I'm an event planner. Can I source vendors through TEDcNOW?** Yes. Planners can source vendors
for their clients and can also join the TEDcNOW professional community. **[CONFIRM B2B terms]**

**I'm a vendor. How do I join?** Thank them and share the vendor registration link {{LINK}}. Do not
run the client intake — tag the chat as VENDOR and hand off to the vendor onboarding team.

**How fast will I hear back?** Within {{4 working hours}}. **[CONFIRM SLA]**

**Will my number be shared with many vendors?** Only with the shortlisted vendors you approve,
and only after your consent. **[CONFIRM process]**

or UPI PIN details.
